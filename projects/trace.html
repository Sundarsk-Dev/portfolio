The problem behind the feature

Personal-data exposure detection can involve adversarial input. An autonomous workflow needs both input handling and controls over what can happen next.

What I designed

I designed a seven-phase pipeline with phase-gated execution and prompt-injection sanitization. The design separates input handling from the decision to advance to the next phase.

A decision with consequences

I made phase progression explicit. Each gate needs acceptance conditions, so autonomous execution also depends on decisions about when work may continue.

Exercise the boundaries

The pipeline was exercised against eight simulated attack scenarios, including attempts to bypass break-glass authorization. The validation presented here is limited to those simulations.
