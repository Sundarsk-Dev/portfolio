The problem behind the feature

A multi-stage investigation involves several kinds of work. Coordinating that work is an architectural concern: when a stage runs, what information it receives, and which boundaries constrain its execution.

The implemented pipeline connects news ingestion, staged investigation, and deterministic guardrails.

What I built

I built an event-driven agentic pipeline for real-time news ingestion and multi-stage autonomous investigations.

The implementation included deterministic guardrails and parallel dispatch across investigation stages.

A decision with consequences

I used parallel dispatch across the investigation workflow. That made coordination between stages a concern of the architecture, alongside the work done inside each stage.

Parallel work introduces decisions about ordering and partial results. These are tradeoffs to examine in this architecture; a throughput improvement is not assumed simply because dispatch is parallel.

Keep orchestration and judgement distinct

The deterministic guardrails address execution constraints. The factual quality of investigation material is a separate evaluation question.

This is the broader engineering thread in the project: coordinating a workflow and making its boundaries explicit are part of building the software.

Evidence and scope

The outcome presented here is the event-driven investigation pipeline. The source link and résumé document the orchestration work; deployment and throughput are not benchmarked in this account.
